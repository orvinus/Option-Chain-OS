0. How the platform fits together

One pipeline, three uses:

  • Live feed (TrueData 1-minute option chain) → TimescaleDB → the analytics tabs and, during market hours, the live orchestrator — the trading brain that evaluates every closed minute.
  • Algo Config edits the LIVE configuration the orchestrator trades with. One document, versioned; the newest version is what trades.
  • Backtesting drives the same orchestrator code over the stored ~6-month archive under a separate sandbox configuration. Nothing done there can affect live trading.

The engines (OI Structure, MTF Ratio, MQAE, Ultra Master Pro) are pure functions — dashboards, live trading and backtests all call the identical code, which is why the platform can promise "what you see is what trades".

1. The six main tabs

1.1 OI Change

The live option-chain dashboard: per-strike OI, OI change since open, LTP, IV and Greeks for the selected symbol/expiry, plus the day's cumulative Call/Put OI-change chart and PCR. The timeframe pills (1m…full-day) change the change-window. Data refreshes over a WebSocket; the "as of" stamp shows the last tick.

1.2 Charts

Total Call vs Put OI over the session for a strike window (ATM ± N), bucketed 1m/5m/…; candles are built client-side from the 1-minute totals. The date picker replays any stored day. Includes drawing tools (trend line, ray, rectangle — persisted per chart) and CSV/Excel export.

1.3 Multi-TF

OI change across all ten timeframes at once (1m, 3m, 5m, 10m, 15m, 30m, 1h, 2h, 3h, full-day) — the table version of "what's building up on each horizon". as_of lets you view any historical minute.

1.4 Ratio

The PCR (Green) and Call/Put Ratio (Yellow) lines over the session — the exact two series the MQAE engine consumes. Same date replay ability.

1.5 Replay

The whole-market replay player: pick a stored day, press ▶ Play, and the option chain, OI-change grids and charts re-play minute by minute. Transport bar: restart ⏮, step ◀ ▶, speed 1–15×, scrubber. No network per playhead move — a day is fetched once.

2. Algo Config — the LIVE control room

This page edits what the engine actually trades. Every edit accumulates in a local draft; nothing reaches the engine until you press Save… and confirm the field-by-field diff. Each save writes a NEW numbered version (v1, v2, …) — the newest version is live. Every save, sign-in and change is recorded in the append-only audit log.

The admin gate

Every /api/algo/* call requires a server-side session (httpOnly cookie). Sign in once; the session survives reloads and backend restarts. If it ever expires mid-use, the page drops back to the sign-in form with an explanation — sign in again and continue.

2.1 Daily Trading Config

  • Live Decision Snapshot (top) — what the orchestrator is doing RIGHT NOW: state (idle/hunting/in-trade/gated/killed), active zone, per-indicator readings, the unanimous direction, position details, gate blocks, today's realized P&L, and the Resume button after a consecutive-loss auto-pause. Polls every 5 s.
  • Expiries strip — all known expiries with weekdays; the traded (current weekly) one highlighted.
  • Kill grid — master kill, 5 day kills, 15 zone kills. One rule for all of them: switched ON while a trade runs, that trade is exited at market the moment you save; while ON no new trade is taken; switched OFF, trading resumes. A zone kill covers the trade that zone opened; a day kill covers today's trade, including one carried overnight.
  • Day tabs → zone rows — per weekday: index symbol, allocation % (or ALL-IN), risk caps (max daily loss %, profit lock %, consecutive-loss pauses), End-Exit toggle, alerts; per zone: start/end times (never overlapping, inside 09:15–15:30), premium band ₹min–₹max, max trades, re-evaluation cadence (zone start vs every candle), strategy active, and the three indicator enable switches with live readings.
  • Indicator names are links (↗) — they open that engine's dashboard bound to the same day/zone.
  • Copy / Reset toolbelt — copy a whole day's settings to other days, copy one zone (including all four engines' parameters) to any day/zone (start/end times are preserved on the target so zones never overlap), reset day/zone/engine to the Appendix-A defaults.

2.2 The four engine dashboards

The honesty rule (read this once): the readings, charts and verdicts on these dashboards are computed by the SERVER from the last saved config. Editing a parameter changes the draft only — the evaluation will NOT move until you press Save All Engine Settings (or the header Save…) and confirm. The panels say this under the editors: "the evaluation below reflects the saved config. Save to apply your edits."

Shared context bar

Zone context (weekday + zone) picks whose parameters run. Expiry defaults to "current (auto)"; pick a past expiry to analyze it. Date replays any stored session; with a date set, the At time freezes the evaluation at that exact minute (the whole window — including the ATM basket — resolves as-of that minute). Live clears both.

2.3 P&L Summary

  • PAPER / LIVE ledger toggle — the two ledgers are never mixed in one number.
  • Cumulative Performance — period P&L, blended return (total P&L ÷ total allocated — never an average of daily %), win rate, avg win/loss, profit factor.
  • Calendar — day cells show net ₹ and % of that day's allocated capital (the same basis the risk limits use). Click a day to filter the trade log. 📝 = a journal note; click the cell (or use the Day Notes card) to read/edit.
  • Trade log — every fill: date/day/zone/side/strike, entry/exit time+price, lots, P&L ₹ and %, exit reason, sub-scenario. Filter by date/zone; Export CSV.

2.4 Paper Trading

Controls: Virtual balance (the simulator's capital, independent of the demat balance), slippage % (applied unfavourably to every fill), simulated latency, fill source, Shadow Mode (mirror LIVE entries into a parallel paper twin), and Reset Paper Session (clears ALL paper trades, restarts the session today — the only sanctioned delete, fully audited).

How to paper trade properly, step by step:
  1. Keep Paper Mode ON (Integrations & Broker → the mode is on by default; the amber ■ PAPER MODE badge shows in the header).
  2. Set your virtual balance here and each weekday's allocation % in Daily Trading Config.
  3. Configure zones/indicators/engines as you want to trade — Save.
  4. During market hours the orchestrator evaluates every closed minute: watch the Live Decision Snapshot. When the enabled indicators agree unanimously, it picks the strike whose premium sits inside the zone's band, hunts a UMP entry, and fills at LTP ± slippage.
  5. Fills, fees (the full ₹17-brokerage + statutory stack) and exits appear in P&L Summary → PAPER and in Telegram alerts.
  6. Reset the session whenever you want a clean slate. Live trades are a separate ledger and are never touched.

The Brokerage Calculator card mirrors the fee engine exactly: enter buy/sell premium + lots and see brokerage ×2, STT (sell side), exchange txn, SEBI, IPFT, GST, stamp duty, the total, net P&L and the breakeven premium move.

2.5 Holiday Calendar

NSE holidays the engine must not trade. Add with the date picker + occasion; remove with ✕. A date listed here hard-kills evaluation for that whole day (state shows "killed / holiday").

2.6 Integrations & Broker

  • Connection rows — feed + broker status. Test Login probes the Lakshmishree Interactive API (audited). Reconcile compares the engine's open live position against the broker's book.
  • Fee model — the editable charge schedule used by paper AND live P&L (they can never disagree).
  • Live Account — Lakshmishree — read-only broker truth: balance highlights (cash available, margin, collateral…), a full field-by-field balance expander, net positions and the day's order book (including rejection reasons). Refresh refetches.
    Weekends/off-hours: the broker gateway itself goes offline; the card then shows a clean one-line reason and — once a successful fetch has happened during market hours — the last snapshot with its timestamp ("⏳ showing the LAST snapshot, fetched …"). Your ₹ funds will appear here the first market morning after they are credited; press Refresh after 09:00 IST Monday.

2.7 Security & Backups

  • Config Lock — "lock during market hours" (only the lock itself may change while the session is open) and "require save confirmation" (OFF = the Save button posts directly without the diff modal).
  • Version history — every saved version with author/time/note; Restore re-saves an old document as a NEW version (the trail never rewinds).
  • Audit log — append-only: sign-ins (including failures), every changed field with old→new, paper resets, notes, backtest runs, kills.

2.8 Validation Summary

The §15 pre-save checklist (zone overlaps, windows inside 09:15–15:30, premium band sanity, max-trades ≥ 1, holiday formats, fees ≥ 0, balances > 0…) and the §14 runtime gate per zone (an incomplete zone can never trade — live or paper — and the block is alerted). Evaluated against the LIVE saved config; the identical functions gate the orchestrator, so this page and the engine can never disagree.

3. Backtesting — the standalone workspace

The idea: a complete copy of the control room, bound to a SANDBOX configuration document, driving the REAL orchestrator over the stored ~6-month archive (2026-02-05 →). Same engines, same unanimous rule, same premium-band strike pick, same UMP entries/exits, same paper fills and fee stack — proven byte-identical to the live path (633/633 series-parity checks; every recorded signal transition re-derived through the live pipeline matches exactly).

4. The trading rules the engine enforces

RuleBehavior
Unanimous rule (§4.1)Whichever indicators are ENABLED must all agree CALL or all PUT; anything else = NO_TRADE. Zero enabled indicators can never trade (§14 blocks + alerts).
Strike-in-bandOn a confirmed side, scan the current weekly's strikes; trade the one whose live premium sits inside the zone's ₹min–₹max band, nearest the band middle; none in band → no trade + one alert.
UMP owns execution (§5.3/§6)Entries come ONLY from the Ultra Master Pro engine's setups (trigger + S1A…S3C / retest R1-R2, two-candle guard, level machinery); exits ONLY from its chain (targets, trail ladder, System B, MAX/BASE SL) or the day-level End-Exit. Filter flips never touch an open trade; a direction flip while hunting discards everything.
Sequencing (§2.3)An open trade suppresses every later zone's entries until it closes. End-Exit (if enabled) force-closes at the LAST zone's end time.
Risk kills (§7)Per day: max-loss auto-kill, profit lock (stop after reaching +X%), consecutive-loss pause (count and/or % — manual Resume required). All recomputed from the ledger, so they survive restarts.
§14 completeness gateA zone missing anything (indicators, band, times…) is dead for the session and the block is alerted — never silent.
§15 validationA config that violates the hard rules cannot be SAVED at all.
Honesty ruleDashboards evaluate the last SAVED config (live page) / SAVED sandbox (workspace) — never unsaved drafts.

5. Data & fidelity

  • Archive: 1-minute option bars for NIFTY + SENSEX from 2026-02-05, all sessions opening exactly 09:15 IST (timezone-repair verified). The live feed's days merge seamlessly on top.
  • Backtest determinism: the same run inputs produce byte-identical results — including across interrupt + resume. Lot sizes and strike steps are frozen into each run.
  • No lookahead: every evaluation uses closed minutes only; the ATM basket, strike membership and the strike picker resolve as-of the simulated instant (this is tested against the live SQL to exact equality).
  • Honest degradations: spot-less sessions (a catalogued vendor gap) run with full-chain baskets exactly as live would and are flagged per day; thin days are skipped by preflight with the reason shown.
  • Fills: 1-minute paper simulation — LTP ± slippage, no queue/liquidity model. Fees are the full statutory stack, identical to live P&L.

6. FAQ / troubleshooting

SymptomExplanation
"I changed settings but nothing changed / it wasn't stored."Two usual causes: (1) the draft was never SAVED — look for the amber "N unsaved changes" and press Save…/Save Sandbox… then Confirm; navigating away or reloading discards drafts. (2) The engine dashboards follow the honesty rule — they evaluate the last saved config, so charts move only after a save. Every successful save shows a green toast "✔ Saved as vN" and appears in Security → version history + audit.
"The broker card shows an error / no funds."Outside market hours the Lakshmishree gateway itself is offline (weekends especially) — the card says so and shows the last snapshot when one exists. Funds credited to the account appear the next market morning; open Integrations & Broker and press Refresh after ~09:00 IST.
"A backtest day was skipped."Open the run's preflight/days table — every skip carries its reason: weekend, config holiday, catalogued bad-data day, excluded by you, no stored data, or too few minutes.
"My backtest made zero trades."Honest outcome: the unanimous rule + premium band + UMP setup requirements are strict. Open any day's replay and watch the signals — you'll see exactly where directions formed and why entries didn't (band empty, disagreement, no UMP setup).
"Two runs differ."Only if their inputs differ — check the config source (sandbox vs live vs version) and settings; each run freezes its document, and identical inputs are proven byte-identical.
"Session expired" mid-useBackend restarted; sign in again (one click — the form appears in place).
