## 1. What changed (by feature)

### Ultra Master Pro — per-zone Trade Entry Timeframe (5 / 15 minutes)
- `UmpEntryModel.entry_timeframe_min: Literal[5,15] = 5` (older saved documents re-validate to 5).
- Engine (`engines/ump/engine.py`): the entry candle window, its confirmed-close minute and the trigger-timeout literal now use the zone's timeframe. **Trigger timeout counts bars of the entry timeframe** (Pine counts chart bars). Key levels (daily / weekly / 1H structure) are untouched. The short last 15-minute window (15:30–15:39) confirms through the existing deferred-close path.
- Chart candles (`replay_ump` → `series.aggregate_minutes`) follow the timeframe; the live forming bar (`live_stream`, `forming_premium_bar`) is bucketed on it and reports `tf_min`.
- UI: "Trade Entry Timeframe" select in the UMP Entry Model card (per zone, all 15 zones); backtest run form override "UMP entry timeframe"; decision-trace zone snapshot carries it.

### Per-entry-kind ON/OFF switches (S1A S1B S1C S2A S2B S3A S3B S3C · R1 R2)
- `UmpEntryModel.scenarios: dict[str,bool]` (missing keys = ON; unknown keys rejected; `UMP_SCENARIOS` constant).
- Enforced once, at `UmpEngine._enter` (before the entry guard). A disabled kind does **not** fall through to the next kind on that tick — the trigger stays armed for a later candle; the Retest loop continues to the next level.
- Warm-up disarm/re-arm now goes through `disarm_entries` / `rearm_entries` (orchestrator + backtest deps), restoring master switches, the scenario map and the timeframe.
- UI: two switch grids under the master switches in the UMP settings card (colours = the chart's marker colours); backtest override pills "UMP entry kinds OFF"; snapshot `scenarios_off`.

### UMP chart display interval incl. 1-second
- `GET /api/algo/engines/ump` gains `interval=` (display only; **every timeframe**: 1s · 5s · 15s · 30s · 1m · 3m · 5m · 10m · 15m · 30m · 1h · 2h · 3h · 1d · entry) and `history=true`. Minute-and-longer candles are session-anchored at 09:15 like TradingView's NSE bars (10m = 09:15, 09:25 …; 1h = 09:15, 10:15 …; 1d = the session); sub-minute candles fold the 1-second live rows of the cursor day.
- `1s` is served for the cursor day from `option_oi_snapshots` (`fetch_premium_seconds`, ≤ 23,100 rows, pure live rows); when the day has no 1-second rows (archive-only / pre-retention) the response falls back to 1-minute with `candles_note`.
- **Fix**: a pinned date without `at` now cuts at that date's own session close (`default_cut_for`) instead of a fixed 15:30.
- Response: `entry_timeframe_min`, `entry_candles` (renamed from `candles_5m`), `candles`, `candles_interval`, `candles_source`, `candles_note`, `candles_window`, `levels_history`, `state_history` (histories only with `history=true`).
- Chart (`UmpTvChart`): interval pills, seconds on the axis below 1 minute, marker snapping to the containing display candle, no zoom reset on interval switch unless nothing is on screen.

### TradingView-style Replay inside the UMP chart
- One fetch per (date, interval) with `history=true`; every playhead move is a pure client-side derivation (`engines/umpReplay.ts::deriveReplayView`): candles truncated, events/trades filtered, the **level ladder and engine state reconstructed** from the engine-recorded histories (`UmpEngine(record_history=True)` — off by default, zero cost elsewhere).
- No-look-ahead rule: an engine fact stamped at minute `ts` is visible only when the playhead's candle end ≥ `ts + 60 s`.
- Replay bar in the UMP panel (Algo Config and Backtesting): date + time → Start; prev/next trading day (holidays skipped, capped at expiry); reused `ReplayController` (play/pause/step/scrub, speeds = bars/second); auto-follow like TV only while the newest bar was on screen.
- `BacktestDayReplay` now uses `levels_history` too (its level ladder previously showed the end-of-day set).
